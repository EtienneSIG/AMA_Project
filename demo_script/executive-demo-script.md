# 🎬 Executive Demo Script — LearnEU Platform (AMA Project)

> **Audience:** Ministry of Education · School District Directors · School Principals · Executive decision-makers
> **Total runtime:** 10 minutes · **Format:** one continuous student journey across four live apps
> **Director:** Executive Demo Director · **Goal:** business impact, not features

---

## 🎯 The single story you will tell

One student — **Lucas, 12, Year 7** — is quietly falling behind in **fractions (maths)**.
You will follow *one thread of evidence* as it travels **Learner → Teacher → Parent → Director**,
and show how the same signal turns into a coordinated intervention and a measurable outcome.

**Cast (seed personas):**
| Role | Persona | Login | App |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learner | **Lucas** (12) | `student@learneu.demo` | Learner Web |
| Teacher | **Mr Klein** | `teacher@learneu.demo` | Teacher Console |
| Parent | **Sophie** (Lucas' mother) | `parent@learneu.demo` | Parent Portal |
| Director | **District office** | `director@learneu.demo` | Director Portal |

**Shared password:** `DemoPass2026!`

---

## ✅ Pre-flight (before the room fills — do NOT do this live)

1. Open **four browser tabs**, one per app, already **logged in**:
   - Learner: `https://app-learner-web-learneu-demo.azurewebsites.net`
   - Teacher: `https://app-teacher-console-learneu-demo.azurewebsites.net`
   - Parent: `https://app-parent-portal-learneu-demo.azurewebsites.net`
   - Director: `https://app-director-portal-learneu-demo.azurewebsites.net`
2. On the Learner tab, pre-select the **Quest dashboard**. On Teacher, pre-open **Learner inbox**.
3. Zoom browser to **125%** so KPIs read from the back of the room.
4. Have the story in your head, not on the screen. Let the product talk.

> **Golden rule:** every click must answer *"so what for a Minister?"* — never narrate the UI.

# STEP 1 — Executive Context

**Goal:** Frame the €-and-equity problem and put a face on it.
**Application:** None (slide or spoken intro) → then the Learner tab on screen, not yet clicked.

**Duration:** 1 minute

**What I show:**
- A single sentence on screen or spoken: *"1 in 4 students falls behind before anyone notices — and by then it costs 3× more to fix."*
- Then bring up the **Learner Web** tab, paused on Lucas' home screen.

**What I click:**
- Nothing yet. Just surface the Learner tab.

**What I say:**
> "Ministers, Directors — the hardest problem in education isn't teaching, it's *timing*. We usually detect a struggling student a full term too late. Meet Lucas, 12. On paper he's fine. In reality he's about to disengage from maths. Watch how our platform catches him — this week, not next term — and how four people around him act as one team."

**Executive message:**
Early detection is the whole game. LearnEU turns a silent risk into a coordinated, in-time response.

**Transition:**
> "Let's start where Lucas starts — his own screen."

**Wow moment:**
The room realises this is not a dashboard demo — it's a *rescue*, told through one child.

---

# STEP 2 — Learner Experience

**Goal:** Show that Lucas gets a personalised, engaging, supportive path — and that the platform is *already* adapting to him.
**Application:** **Learner Web** (`student@learneu.demo`)

**Duration:** 2.5 minutes

**Navigation path:** Left rail → **Quest dashboard** → **Test your knowledge** → **Ask your teacher** → **My progress**

**What I click:**
1. **Quest dashboard** (🎯) — the gamified home.
2. **Test your knowledge** (🧠) — attempt a fractions item; open the **Explanation** drawer.
3. **Ask your teacher** (💬) — show the AI tutor answer + "Ask your teacher" thread.
4. **My progress** (📈) — expand the **Fractions** chapter card.

**What I show:**
- **Quest dashboard:** streak, badges, today's quest — engagement, not pressure.
- **Test your knowledge:** the adaptive picker serves an item at *exactly* Lucas' level (target ~70% success), and the retractable Explanation drawer gives an age-appropriate hint.
- **Ask your teacher:** the AI tutor answers instantly *and* routes a real question to Mr Klein — the human stays in the loop.
- **My progress:** mastery grouped **by chapter** — Fractions is visibly lagging behind the other chapters.

**What I say:**
> "Lucas doesn't see a test — he sees a quest. But underneath, the platform is doing something powerful: every item is chosen for *him*, at the edge of what he can do. When he's stuck, he gets a hint and can ask his real teacher in one tap — the AI never grades him alone. And here — 'My progress' — the platform has quietly flagged that Fractions is slipping. Lucas can see it. Now watch who else can."

**Executive message:**
Personalised, motivating, and safe: adaptive learning that keeps a human teacher in control and makes a struggle *visible* the moment it starts.

**Transition:**
> "That fractions signal doesn't stay on Lucas' screen. It's already on his teacher's desk."

**Wow moment:**
Executives see engagement (a game) and rigour (level-perfect items + early flag) in the *same* screen — with a teacher override, not autonomous grading.

---

# STEP 3 — Teacher Intervention

**Goal:** Prove the teacher is handed a *prioritised, explained* action — not another dashboard to decode.
**Application:** **Teacher Console** (`teacher@learneu.demo`)

**Duration:** 2 minutes

**Navigation path:** Left rail → **Learner inbox** → **Class dashboard** → **Adaptive paths** → **Assessment**

**What I click:**
1. **Learner inbox** (📥) — Lucas is near the top with a risk badge.
2. **Class dashboard** (🧑‍🏫) — class heatmap; Fractions column is red for a small group incl. Lucas.
3. **Adaptive paths** (🧭) — the platform's **recommended next step** for Lucas.
4. **Assessment** (📝) — recent short-answer result with the AI draft + **teacher override**.

**What I show:**
- **Learner inbox:** Lucas surfaced automatically with a **risk indicator** — the teacher doesn't hunt for him.
- **Class dashboard:** learning analytics at a glance — who, which skill, how urgent.
- **Adaptive paths:** a concrete **recommended action** (a targeted fractions catch-up set) the teacher can approve in one click.
- **Assessment:** AI proposes a grade + feedback; **Mr Klein confirms or edits** — accountability stays human.

**What I say:**
> "Here's the difference for a teacher. Mr Klein doesn't analyse 30 students — the platform hands him the *three* who need him today, with the reason. For Lucas it even proposes the intervention: a short, targeted fractions path. Mr Klein approves it with one click. And every AI suggestion — including grading — waits for his sign-off. The teacher's time moves from *finding* problems to *solving* them."

**Executive message:**
Teacher productivity and equity in one move: the system triages and recommends; the teacher decides. Less admin, earlier help, no black-box grading.

**Transition:**
> "Mr Klein approves the plan — and instantly, the person who matters most at home is looped in."

**Wow moment:**
"Recommended action, one-click approve, human override" lands as *this respects my teachers* — the fear of AI replacing educators evaporates.

# STEP 4 — Parent Engagement

**Goal:** Show parents get clarity and a concrete way to help — in their own language — without data overload.
**Application:** **Parent Portal** (`parent@learneu.demo`)

**Duration:** 1.5 minutes

**Navigation path:** Left rail → **My child's progress** → **Weekly digest** → **Messages**

**What I click:**
1. **My child's progress** (🧒) — mastery by chapter + recent activity.
2. **Weekly digest** (📊) — "This week at a glance" with a plain-language summary.
3. **Messages** (✉️) — Mr Klein's note about the fractions plan (moderated before delivery).

**What I show:**
- **My child's progress:** Sophie sees the *same* fractions signal — no jargon, no scores dump.
- **Weekly digest:** a friendly weekly summary **plus a "How to help" tip** tied to fractions.
- **Messages:** a two-way, safety-moderated channel with the teacher — Sophie can reply.

**What I say:**
> "Now Lucas' mother, Sophie. She doesn't get a spreadsheet — she gets a weekly story in her own language, and one concrete thing she can do this week to help with fractions. And she can message Mr Klein directly, on a channel that's checked for safety before anything is delivered. For the first time, home and school are pushing in the same direction, at the same time."

**Executive message:**
Parent involvement becomes structural, not occasional — and GDPR-safe by design (consent-gated, language-localised, moderated).

**Transition:**
> "So Lucas, Mr Klein and Sophie are aligned. Now — how does a Director know this is working across 40 schools?"

**Wow moment:**
"A tip she can actually use, in her language, safely" reframes parent apps from *reporting* to *partnership*.

---

# STEP 5 — Director Oversight

**Goal:** Elevate from one child to system-wide steering — with privacy built in.
**Application:** **Director Portal** (`director@learneu.demo`)

**Duration:** 2 minutes

**Navigation path:** **Reports** (default page) → **Scope** card → **Multi-school hierarchy** → **District dashboard**

**What I click:**
1. Land on **Reports** — the embedded **EU-resident analytics** view (KPIs).
2. **Scope** card — shows the director's *approved* remit (which schools/regions).
3. **Multi-school hierarchy** → pick a **District node** → **District dashboard**.
4. (Optional) **Hierarchical report** — district → region → national roll-up.

**What I show:**
- **School-wide KPIs:** outcome-gap trend, engagement, intervention volume.
- **Population insights:** which cohorts/skills are moving — Fractions improving where the intervention ran.
- **Intervention effectiveness:** before/after on the targeted group.
- **Privacy guardrails on screen:** *"Cohort minimum disclosure (≥10)"* and *"re-identification screening… No learner-level data is shown."*

**What I say:**
> "At the top, the Director never sees Lucas by name — and that's the point. She sees that the fractions intervention we just watched is *bending the outcome-gap curve* across the district, and where to send resources next. Cohorts under ten are automatically suppressed; re-identification is screened before anything renders. This is board-grade, ministry-grade reporting — powerful *because* it's privacy-safe, all hosted in the EU."

**Executive message:**
Data-driven decisions and resource allocation at scale — with EU residency and child-data protection enforced by the platform, not by policy alone.

**Transition:**
> "Notice what just happened: one signal from one child became a district-level decision — without a single spreadsheet or a single privacy breach."

**Wow moment:**
"The Director sees the impact but *cannot* see the child" — privacy and insight are not a trade-off. Ministry heads exhale.

# STEP 6 — Closed-Loop Impact

**Goal:** Make the flywheel explicit in one breath.
**Application:** Spoken, over a simple 4-node visual (or gesture across the four tabs).

**Duration:** 0.5 minute

**What I show:**
- A single flow: **Learner → Teacher → Parent → Director → back to Learner.**

**What I click:**
- Nothing — or flip quickly across the four tabs to reinforce "same story, four seats."

**What I say:**
> "One thread. Lucas' screen *detected* it. Mr Klein *acted* on it. Sophie *reinforced* it at home. The Director *scaled* the decision — and the next path Lucas sees is better because of it. Four stakeholders, one loop, closing in days instead of a term. Each person did *less* work and got a *better* result."

**Executive message:**
LearnEU is not four apps — it's one closed loop where every stakeholder amplifies the others around the student.

**Transition:**
> "Let me leave you with what that loop delivers."

**Wow moment:**
The audience sees the *system*, not the screens — the "aha" that this is an operating model, not a tool.

# STEP 7 — Executive Summary

**Goal:** Convert the story into five board-level outcomes and a decision.
**Application:** Closing slide (or spoken).

**Duration:** 0.5 minute

**What I show:**
- Five outcomes, one line each:
  1. **Better student outcomes** — struggles caught in-week, not next term (outcome-gap −26% target).
  2. **Higher engagement** — learning framed as a quest; adaptive to each child.
  3. **Increased parent involvement** — weekly, actionable, in-language, GDPR-safe.
  4. **Improved teacher productivity** — triage + recommendations; admin time −45% target.
  5. **Data-driven decisions** — district-to-national steering, EU-resident, privacy-by-design.

**What I say:**
> "Better outcomes, higher engagement, real parent partnership, teachers freed to teach, and decisions grounded in privacy-safe evidence. All EU-hosted, all with a human in the loop, all demonstrated live today — not on a roadmap. The question isn't whether this works. It's how fast we put it in front of your students."

**Executive message:**
Proven, live, compliant, and human-centred — ready to scale from one child to a national system.

**Transition:**
> "I'd love to walk your team through a pilot in one district next term."

**Wow moment:**
The close reframes the ask from *"evaluate a product"* to *"schedule a rollout"* — the meeting ends on momentum.

## 🛟 If something is slow (recovery lines)

- **App still waking (~90s cold start):** *"While this warms up — remember, everything you're seeing is EU-hosted and consent-gated by design."* Keep talking to the story, not the spinner.
- **A tab errors:** switch to the next stakeholder tab; the story is resilient — the *loop* is the message, not any single screen.
- **Question on AI risk:** *"Every AI action here waits for a teacher's sign-off — no autonomous grading, no learner data outside the EU."*
