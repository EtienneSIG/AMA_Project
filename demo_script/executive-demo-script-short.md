# ⚡ Executive Demo — Short Version (5 min) — LearnEU

> **Audience:** Ministry / District Directors / Principals · **Runtime:** 5 min · **Story:** one student, four apps
> **Cast:** Learner **Lucas** (12) · Teacher **Mr Klein** · Parent **Sophie** · **District** office
> **Logins:** `student|teacher|parent|director@learneu.demo` · pw `DemoPass2026!`
> **Pre-flight:** 4 tabs open & logged in, browser at 125%. Never narrate the UI — answer *"so what?"*

---

## STEP 1 — Context (30s)
**App:** — (spoken) · **Show:** Learner home, paused.
**Say:** *"1 in 4 students falls behind before anyone notices — costing 3× more to fix. Meet Lucas, 12. On paper he's fine; he's about to disengage from maths. Watch four people catch him this week, as one team."*
**Message:** Early detection is the whole game.
**Wow:** This is a rescue, not a dashboard tour.
→ *"It starts on his screen."*

## STEP 2 — Learner (1m15)
**App:** Learner Web · **Path:** Quest dashboard → Test your knowledge → My progress
**Click:** 🎯 Quest → 🧠 attempt a fractions item + open Explanation → 📈 expand the **Fractions** card.
**Show:** a game (streak/quest) on top of a level-perfect adaptive item; "My progress" already flags Fractions slipping.
**Say:** *"Lucas sees a quest — underneath, every item is chosen for him, and the platform has quietly flagged fractions. He can ask his real teacher in one tap; AI never grades him alone."*
**Message:** Personalised, motivating, human-in-the-loop.
**Wow:** Engagement + rigour + early flag on one screen.
→ *"That signal is already on his teacher's desk."*

## STEP 3 — Teacher (1m15)
**App:** Teacher Console · **Path:** Learner inbox → Adaptive paths → Assessment
**Click:** 📥 Lucas at top with risk badge → 🧭 approve the **recommended fractions catch-up** → 📝 confirm/edit an AI-drafted grade.
**Show:** auto-triage (the 3 students who need him today), a one-click recommended intervention, teacher override on grading.
**Say:** *"Mr Klein doesn't analyse 30 students — the system hands him the three who matter, with the reason and a proposed fix. He approves in one click. Every AI suggestion waits for his sign-off."*
**Message:** Less admin, earlier help, no black-box grading.
**Wow:** "Recommend, one-click, human override" — AI respects teachers.
→ *"He approves — and home is looped in instantly."*

## STEP 4 — Parent (45s)
**App:** Parent Portal · **Path:** My child's progress → Weekly digest → Messages
**Click:** 🧒 same fractions signal → 📊 "This week" + a **How-to-help** tip → ✉️ moderated note from the teacher.
**Show:** plain-language weekly story in Sophie's language, one concrete action, a safety-checked two-way channel.
**Say:** *"Sophie gets a weekly story in her language and one thing she can do this week — and a safe channel to the teacher. Home and school finally push the same way, at the same time."*
**Message:** Parent partnership, GDPR-safe by design.
**Wow:** From reporting → real partnership.
→ *"So how does a Director know this works across 40 schools?"*

## STEP 5 — Director (1m)
**App:** Director Portal · **Path:** Reports → Scope → Multi-school hierarchy → District dashboard
**Click:** land on EU-resident KPIs → **Scope** (approved remit) → pick a district → **District dashboard**.
**Show:** outcome-gap trend bending where the intervention ran; on-screen guardrails *"cohort ≥10, re-identification screened, no learner-level data."*
**Say:** *"The Director never sees Lucas by name — that's the point. She sees the fractions intervention bending the outcome-gap curve, and where to send resources next. Privacy-safe, EU-hosted."*
**Message:** Data-driven, resource-smart steering with child-data protection enforced by the platform.
**Wow:** Sees the impact but *cannot* see the child.
→ *"One child's signal became a district decision — no spreadsheet, no breach."*

## STEP 6 — Close (45s)
**App:** spoken over a 4-node loop.
**Say:** *"One thread: Lucas' screen detected it, Mr Klein acted, Sophie reinforced it, the Director scaled it — in days, not a term. Everyone did less and got more. That's five outcomes: better results, higher engagement, real parent involvement, teacher time back, and privacy-safe decisions — all EU-hosted, all live today. The question isn't whether it works — it's how fast we pilot it in one district next term."*
**Message:** One closed loop around the student; proven, compliant, ready to scale.
**Wow:** Ends on "schedule a rollout," not "evaluate a product."
