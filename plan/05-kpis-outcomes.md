# 05 — KPIs & Expected Outcomes

## North-star outcomes (from the case study)

| # | KPI | Baseline | Target | Cadence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Outcome gap between high- and low-performing schools | 40% | **−26% (≈ 14.8pp absolute reduction)** | Monthly per market | Learning Sciences + RAI |
| K2 | Teacher administrative time | 35% of working hours | **−45%** | Quarterly survey + telemetry | Assessment AI WS |
| K3 | Content localisation cycle (per market entry) | 8–12 months | **6 weeks** | Per content batch | Localisation WS |
| K4 | GDPR Art. 8 compliance | n/a | **100% maintained, 0 breaches** | Continuous + monthly review | DPO |
| K5 | EU AI Act conformity | n/a | **CE-marked before P3 exit; 0 serious incidents** | Continuous + quarterly PMM | AI Act CO |

## Leading indicators (per workstream)

### Adaptive learner model (drives K1)
- Mastery improvement per learner per term (controlled vs. control group)
- Coverage breadth (avoid filter-bubble): % of curriculum exposed
- Engagement (time-on-task) — tracked client-side, aggregated
- Per-cohort fairness disparity (gating metric)

### Assessment AI (drives K2)
- # auto-gradable assignment types deployed
- Teacher minutes saved per assignment (instrumented)
- Teacher override rate (target ≤ 10% steady-state)
- Calibration error (ECE) ≤ 0.05

### Localisation (drives K3)
- Throughput: units localised / week / market
- First-pass acceptance rate (no rewrite needed)
- Reviewer cycle time
- Glossary coverage % per subject

### Compliance (drives K4 & K5)
- % features with current Annex IV file
- % markets with current DPIA (< 12 months)
- Erasure request mean cycle time
- # of overrides exercised (proves Art. 14 oversight is live, not theatrical)

### Adoption (enabler)
- # schools onboarded per market per month
- Teacher trust survey (NPS) per quarter
- Parent portal active usage

## Reporting cadence
- **Weekly** — workstream status, RAI dashboard
- **Bi-weekly** — Architecture Review Board, RAI Council
- **Monthly** — Steering Committee (KPIs + risks + spend)
- **Quarterly** — RAI re-evaluation report, Post-Market Monitoring report (AI Act Art. 72)
- **Annually** — DPIA refresh, Records of Processing review
